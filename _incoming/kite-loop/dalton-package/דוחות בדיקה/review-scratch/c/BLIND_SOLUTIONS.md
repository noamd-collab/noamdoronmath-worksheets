## Q1 (ABCD, AB=BC, CD=DA)
- a. Two separate pairs of adjacent equal sides → kite. Head vertices B, D (where the equal sides meet); side vertices A, C; main diagonal BD, secondary AC.
- b. Errors: (1) the angle ∠ABD is not included between the two sides used (AB, AD), so it is not SAS; (2) circular: "main diagonal bisects the head angle" is a consequence of the congruence being proven. Correct: SSS (AB=CB, AD=CD, BD common) → ∠BAD=∠BCD.
- c. ∠BAD = (360−70−50)/2 = 120°.
- d. From the congruence ∠ABM=∠CBM; △ABM≅△CBM (SAS) → AM=MC, ∠AMB=∠CMB=90°. M is not the midpoint of BD: if BM=MD then △ABM≅△ADM (SAS, right angle, AM common) → ∠ABM=∠ADM, but 35°≠25°.

## Q2 (KLMN, KL=KN, ML=MN, KS=4, SM=9)
- a. KM is the perpendicular bisector of LN (or △KPL≅△KPN SAS) → PL=PN.
- b. QK²=QS²+16, QM²=QS²+81 → QM>QK, QK≠QM.
- c. KM: reflection in KM swaps L,N (KM ⊥ LN, S midpoint). LN axis ⇔ SK=SM ⇔ LN is the perp. bisector of KM ⇔ KL=ML ⇔ rhombus. KLMN: 4≠9 → not a rhombus, LN not an axis.

## Q3 (AB=AD, CB=CD, ∠BAD=2x, ∠BCD=x, ∠ABC=3x)
- a. △ABC≅△ADC (SSS) → ∠ADC=∠ABC. 9x=360 → x=40; A=80°, C=40°, B=D=120°.
- b. ∠ABD=50°, ∠CBD=70°.
- c. 50≠70 so BD does not bisect ∠ABC; only the main diagonal AC bisects the head angles.
- d. ∠BPC = ∠BAP+∠ABP = 40+25 = 65°.

## Q4 (heads A,C; AB=15, CB=20, BD=24)

## Q5
- a. EG is the perpendicular bisector of FH → EF=EH, GF=GH; heads E, G; main diagonal EG.
- b. Sides 5, √90, 15, √160 cm; no two pairs of adjacent equal sides → not a kite. Perpendicular diagonals alone are not enough.
- c. Contrapositive: every kite has perpendicular diagonals (proved by congruence), so non-perpendicular → not a kite.

## Q7 (AB=AD=3, CB=CD=4)

## Q13 (sufficiency)
- a. Sufficient (ASA on AC).
- b. Sufficient: ∠ABD=∠ADB, subtract → ∠CBD=∠CDB → CB=CD.
- c. Sufficient: in isosceles △ABD the altitude from A is the perp. bisector of BD → CB=CD.
- d. Not sufficient (SSA): e.g. C at origin, bisector on the x-axis, rays ±30°, A=(10,0), radius 6: B on one ray at the far intersection, D on the other at the near one — convex, AB=AD, CA bisects ∠BCD, CB≠CD.
- Figure drawn as a symmetric kite for all four items; in d the answer is "not sufficient".

## Q16 ★ (∠ABC=90°, AO=p, OC=q, BO=r)
- a. (p+q)² = AB²+CB² = p²+q²+2r² → r²=pq.
- b. p+q=2r → (p−q)²=(p+q)²−4pq=0 → p=q=r → all sides equal, all angles 90° → square.
- c. right angle only: p=1, q=4, r=2; equal diagonals only: p=1, q=5, r=3.
