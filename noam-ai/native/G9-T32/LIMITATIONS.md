# G9-T32 — native NoamAI export scope

The three canonical noam-ai.json files and their referenced SVG figures are included byte-for-byte. Their current hashes are bound by the final independent level reports and the root delivery record. Editable sources, sheet.json, answer keys and original source PDFs are private.

The current native schema does not express every similarity proof or perimeter-ratio demand as a typed theorem/ratio claim. Exact descriptive goals preserve those student demands. In level A, the base-ratio goal in question20 is descriptive because the native cross-object equal-value rule rejects the otherwise equivalent typed ratio; the given ratio and typed unknown base lengths remain. These are documented schema limitations, not omitted worksheet questions.

This delivery certifies the printed content and the exported data reviewed in the independent reports. It does not certify a live consumer implementation of every highlight or auxiliary action, publish a site, or authorize a release. A later integration must retain these limits and validate the actions its consumer actually offers.

Native geometry contains exact coordinates under the existing export contract and can permit mathematical inference. No non-inferability guarantee is made.
