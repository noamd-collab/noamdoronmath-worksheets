# Google sign-in icon

`google-g-logo.png` is the unmodified official Google G asset downloaded on 2026-09-08 from:
https://developers.google.com/static/identity/images/g-logo.png

Branding reference: https://developers.google.com/identity/branding-guidelines

The Wix entry uses the icon at its original aspect ratio on a white sign-in button, with localized action text. It links to the existing optional learning account at `learning.html?signin=google`.
