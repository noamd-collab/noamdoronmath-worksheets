## Film: fraction-division-meaning-grade-6  (משמעות חילוק שברים, grade 6)
Teach division of fractions as MEASUREMENT: "how many times does the part fit in the whole". Two examples in ONE loop, on bars/number line: 
 (A) 3/4 : 1/4 = 3 — a bar of length 1 whole split in 4 equal quarters, the first 3 quarters shaded (that is 3/4); jump/mark one quarter at a time counting 1, 2, 3 (three 1/4 pieces fit in 3/4). 
 (B) 2 : 1/2 = 4 — two whole bars (or a number line 0..2), each split in halves, count 1, 2, 3, 4 halves; so 4 halves fit in 2.
Top strip (fixed, Hebrew, no equation): `כמה פעמים נכנס החלק?`. Division sign is ":" . Fractions must be drawn STACKED (numerator over bar over denominator), in SVG and in the formula row (use the cl-frac HTML). Counting numbers 1,2,3 / 1,2,3,4 appear above/below each piece in big digits. Bars must be accurately proportioned (quarters exactly equal; halves exactly equal; same unit length in both examples so the bars align with the 560 viewBox). Layout: example A in the upper half, example B in the lower half of the viewBox (e.g. viewBox '0 0 560 380'), appearing in sequence. Formula chunks in order: `3/4 : 1/4 = 3` (stacked fractions), ✓-free first result; then `2 : 1/2 = 4`; final ✓ chunk after the second. Result digits 3 and 4 in green once each at their reveal. Duration about 14–15 s. Page context says "חילוק שברים שואל כמה פעמים החלק נכנס לשלם" and mentions that dividing by a fraction smaller than 1 makes the result bigger.
