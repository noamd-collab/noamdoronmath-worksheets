## Film: distributive-area-model-grade-4  (חוק הפילוג ומכפלות חלקיות, grade 4)
Teach: the area model of multiplication — 12 × 4 is a rectangle 12 units wide and 4 units tall; cut it at 10 into 10 × 4 and 2 × 4, find each area (40 and 8), then ADD the areas: 12 × 4 = 10 × 4 + 2 × 4 = 40 + 8 = 48.
Suggested beats: (1) rectangle drawn as exactly 12 columns × 4 rows of unit squares (use a light grid, `cl-grid`), width labeled 12 above, height labeled 4 at the side; top strip: `12 × 4 = ?` (ltr:true) or a Hebrew question; (2) a dashed cut after column 10 splits it into a blue 10×4 part and an orange 2×4 part (the parts may separate a little); (3) areas: 10 × 4 = 40 on the big part, 2 × 4 = 8 on the small part (labels must fit at ≥30 units — put the small one above/below with a short leader if needed); (4) both parts slide back together and 40 + 8 = 48 appears, 48 in green with ✓. Formula chunks, in order: `12 × 4`, `= (10 + 2) × 4`, `= 10 × 4 + 2 × 4`, `= 40 + 8 = 48`, then the ✓ chunk. Duration about 12–13 s. Page context: grade 4 page text says "חוק הפילוג: כופלים כל חלק בנפרד ומחברים".
