## Film: measurement-geometry-synthesis-grade-6  (מדידות, גאומטריה ופתרון בכמה שלבים, grade 6)
Teach a multi-step composite-area problem, grade-6 level (NOT a plain rectangle): an L-shaped floor = a 10 × 6 rectangle (cm) with a 4 × 3 corner cut out of the top-right corner. Dimensions: bottom side 10 ס״מ, left side 6 ס״מ, the cut-out is 4 ס״מ wide and 3 ס״מ tall (so the top side is 6 ס״מ, the step down is 3 ס״מ, the right side is 3 ס״מ). Draw the L shape to scale (e.g. 1 cm = 30 units → 300 × 180, notch 120 × 90) with dimension labels 10, 6, 4, 3 (units in the first label at least, e.g. `10 ס״מ`; others digits only with a note "כל המידות בס״מ" ONLY if it is ≥ 28 units; otherwise put the unit on each).
Plan in 3 numbered steps (each step shown with a big step badge 1 / 2 / 3 in a circle, and its result chip):
 Step 1 — complete the L to the full rectangle with a dashed outline: 10 × 6 = 60 סמ״ר.
 Step 2 — the missing corner: 4 × 3 = 12 סמ״ר (highlight it in orange).
 Step 3 — subtract: 60 − 12 = 48 סמ״ר; the L fills in blue and the answer `48 סמ״ר` turns green once.
Top strip (fixed Hebrew): `מה שטח הצורה?`. Formula chunks in order: `10 × 6 = 60 סמ״ר`, `4 × 3 = 12 סמ״ר`, `60 − 12 = 48 סמ״ר`, then the ✓ chunk. The viewBox may be taller, e.g. '0 0 560 400'. Duration about 14–16 s. Units always shown on area results (סמ״ר) and on lengths (ס״מ).
